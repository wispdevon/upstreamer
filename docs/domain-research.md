# Domain shortlist

Research date: 17 October 2026. No registration or purchase was performed.

1. **upstream.guide** — strongest match for the editorial field guide. [Registry RDAP](https://rdap.identitydigital.services/rdap/domain/upstream.guide) returned no registration record during research.
2. **upstream.page** — keeps the brand with a lower published extension price. [Registry RDAP](https://pubapi.registry.google/rdap/domain/upstream.page) returned no registration record.
3. **learnupstream.dev** — clear learning/developer positioning. [Registry RDAP](https://pubapi.registry.google/rdap/domain/learnupstream.dev) returned no registration record.
4. **mergewithcare.dev** — distinctive and aligned with the tone, though it may suggest the app merges code. [Registry RDAP](https://pubapi.registry.google/rdap/domain/mergewithcare.dev).
5. **forkforward.dev** — memorable; forkforward.com is already registered. [Registry RDAP](https://pubapi.registry.google/rdap/domain/forkforward.dev).

No registration record does not prove availability: a registrar must confirm reserved names and exact premium/renewal pricing. [Cloudflare's extension price list](https://pricing.registrar.cloudflare.com/) listed .guide at $32.20/year, .page at $10.20/year, and .dev at $12.20/year during research; these are not quotes for the exact names. [.dev requires HTTPS](https://www.registry.google/domains/dev/).

Upstream is a crowded name: unrelated products use [upstream.so](https://upstream.so/about/), [upstream.do](https://www.upstream.do/for-agents), and [upstream.io](https://upstream.io/company/). This is an initial conflict scan, not trademark clearance. Avoid opensource.guide, which is already [GitHub's Open Source Guides](https://opensource.guide/).
